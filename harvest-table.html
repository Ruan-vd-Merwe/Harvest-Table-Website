The Concept

Not a garden.
A guest journey.

DoubleTree already has the architectural ingredients — roof decks, courtyards, tiled corridors and planted pockets. Harvest Roof can convert these into a premium guest experience while producing microgreens, herbs, strawberries and edible flowers that move directly into the hotel's kitchen and rooms.


This is not an industrial operation. It is a calm, beautiful, productive space that gives the hotel a genuine local story — and gives guests something to talk about when they leave.

01
Arrival threshold
Guests move from the hotel lobby into a planted transition space — a green threshold rather than a dead corridor.
02
The garden tunnel
A lightweight vertical garden tunnel with warm lighting, herbs, flowers and hydroponic columns. The tunnel tells the Harvest Roof story without feeling like a farm shed.
03
The strawberry moment
A small raised strawberry section lets guests pick one or two non-pesticide berries during a hosted experience, family stay or conference break.
04
Sit in the flower garden
Guests sit among flowers and edible planting — a quiet coffee corner, a pre-dinner space, a photo moment, or a small chef-hosted tasting.
05
Eat the story
Microgreens, herbs, edible flowers and berries appear on breakfast, cocktails, desserts and conference platters — giving the hotel a local-produce narrative with every plate.

What We Grow

Four crops.
One coherent story.

Each crop is chosen for its visual quality, culinary use, and ability to move quickly from garden to room or plate.

Cut Flowers
Flowers & Botanicals
Grown on-site from seasonal Cape varieties. Used in rooms, at reception, on dining tables, and as pillow gifts. Eliminates delivery delays and gives the hotel a visible growing story.
Room amenities · Turndown gifting · F&B garnish · Conference tables
Specialty Berry
Strawberries
Non-pesticide, raised planters. Small batches of exceptional quality. Used for guest gifting, breakfast service, desserts and the signature pick-your-own activation experience.
Guest gifting · Breakfast · Dessert · Pick-your-own activation
Specialty Greens
Microgreens
Harvested within 24 hours of delivery. Sunflower, pea shoots, radish, amaranth and more — chosen by the chef based on the week's menu. The highest-value crop per square metre, and visually striking on the plate.
Chef use only · Harvested to order · 24-hour delivery

Fresh-cut and living herb pots available weekly. Basil, thyme, rosemary, mint, lemon verbena, edible flowers. Can be placed in suites as a guest amenity or supplied to the kitchen on a standing order.
Kitchen standing order · Suite amenity · Event garnish

The Partnership

Three ways
to work together.

We propose starting with the simplest model that lets both parties learn before committing to anything larger.

Starter
Produce-share model
Harvest Roof grows and sells microgreens, herbs, berries and edible flowers to the hotel at agreed pricing. The hotel receives a garden amenity and fresh supply, with no service fee.
  • Lowest risk entry point for the hotel
  • No monthly commitment required
  • Volume and crop selection agreed weekly
  • Hotel pays per delivery at set price per tray or bunch
Amenity
Garden as a service
DoubleTree pays a monthly service fee for the garden, its maintenance and guest-ready presentation. Produce up to a monthly allowance is included. Best if the hotel values the amenity and brand story.
  • Predictable monthly cost for the hotel
  • Harvest Roof manages all planting and upkeep
  • Produce allowance included in fee
  • Guest-ready presentation at all times
Recommended
Hybrid pilot model
A smaller monthly retainer covers maintenance, while produce sales and guest activations create upside for both parties. We recommend this as the starting model for the 8–12 week pilot.
  • Hotel approves one guest-facing zone and one propagation area
  • Harvest Roof earns from produce sales and a modest service fee
  • Hotel receives the garden, the guest experience and the story
  • After 8–12 weeks, both parties review and decide on expansion
Optional Add-on
Guest activations
Strawberry-picking moments, microgreen tastings, chef garden tours and conference break experiences. Priced and operated separately — can be added to any of the above models.
  • Adds experiential revenue and marketing content
  • Particularly strong for conference and family segments
  • Can be scheduled around hotel programming

Next Steps

How we
get started.

We propose a focused five-step process to move from this proposal to a live pilot — with every structural and operational assumption confirmed before any installation begins.

01
Walk the spaces together
Identify the strongest guest-facing route through the rooftop, courtyard and corridor areas with Francois and the hotel team.
02
Confirm structural information
Request as-built drawings or roof loading data to validate assumptions about structural capacity, waterproofing and drainage before any installation.
03
Mark the pilot zones
Designate the flower and strawberry guest garden, hydroponic herb rails, microgreen propagation area and compost workflow location.
04
Price the 8–12 week pilot
Agree on the partnership model, installation scope, produce pricing and activation schedule for the initial pilot period.
05
Measure and expand
At the end of the pilot, review guest response, produce yield, menu usage and operational effort together — then decide what to grow next.
